**Functional Requirements**

1)The system shall accept seven inputs: age group, gender, symptom 1, symptom 2, duration, fever, and chest pain.

2)The system shall suggest an urgency level of Low, Medium, or High.

3)Each suggestion shall be sent to a doctor, and the doctor shall make the final decision.

4)Every decision shall be recorded in a log.

**Non-Functional Requirements**

1)The model shall be explainable and shall use a Decision Tree.

2)The system shall use only synthetic data and no real patient data.

3)The system is intended for educational purposes only and is not for clinical use.

4)Every High-urgency case shall be reviewed by a human.
